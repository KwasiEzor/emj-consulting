Create a modern, premium and highly animated multi-page travel agency website called "EMJ-Consulting".

STYLE & DESIGN
- Apple-inspired design language.
- Elegant glassmorphism effects.
- Luxury and professional appearance.
- Dark blue (#0B1F3A), white, gold accents (#D4AF37), and subtle gradients.
- Smooth micro-interactions and premium animations.
- Framer Motion style animations.
- Large rounded corners.
- High-end typography (Inter, Poppins).
- Full HD images and cinematic backgrounds.
- Responsive on mobile, tablet and desktop.
- Excellent accessibility and SEO.
